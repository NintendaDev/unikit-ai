# EXP-005: Loot pop

## 1. Identification
- ID: EXP-005
- Title: Loot pop
- Summary: A bright pop when loot drops, to see whether players notice rewards more often.
- Category: feedback
- Status: Ready for Planning
- Version: 1
- Created: 2026-10-01
- Updated: 2026-10-01
- Related: none
- Origin: none

## 2. Problem
Players walk past loot without noticing it.

## 3. Hypothesis
If a bright pop marks every loot drop, players notice rewards more often, because the reward is visible.

## 4. What We Test
Do players notice loot drops more often with a bright pop?

## 5. Design
When an enemy is defeated, the loot appears with a short bright flash and a rising sound. In the other variant it simply appears.
The card describes a disposable prototype, not production-ready functionality.

## 6. Gameplay Flow
The player defeats an enemy and sees the loot. The goal is to collect it, the progress is the loot counter, success is a collected item, failure is a missed item and the next action is the next enemy.

## 7. Variants
A: no pop. B: a bright pop

## 8. Interface and Feedback
A flash around the loot and the existing counter.

## 9. Minimum Scope
### Must Have
- The flash; a switch between variant A and variant B.
### Can Be Simplified
- The exact colour and length of the flash.
### Out of Scope
- New loot types; a drop table.

## 10. How to Test
Six players, two rounds each, about ten minutes; notes are collected in a chat.

## 11. Success Criteria
Players collect more of the dropped items in variant B (a preliminary acceptance target).

## 12. Risks
The flash may be so strong that it hides the loot.

## 13. Follow-up Decisions
Supported: keep the pop. Partially Supported: tune its size. Not Supported: drop it. Inconclusive: repeat.
