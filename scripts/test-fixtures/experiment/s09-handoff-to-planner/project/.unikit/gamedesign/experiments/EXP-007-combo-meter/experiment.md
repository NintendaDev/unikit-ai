# EXP-007: Combo meter

## 1. Identification
- ID: EXP-007
- Title: Combo meter
- Summary: A meter that shows the current combo, to see whether players chain actions more often.
- Category: feedback
- Status: Draft
- Version: 1
- Created: 2026-10-01
- Updated: 2026-10-01
- Related: none
- Origin: none

## 2. Problem
Players do not notice that chaining actions gives a bonus.

## 3. Hypothesis
If a meter shows the combo, players chain actions more often, because the chain becomes visible.

## 4. What We Test
Do players chain actions more often when a meter shows the combo?

## 5. Design
A bar under the score fills with each chained action and empties when the chain breaks.
The card describes a disposable prototype, not production-ready functionality.

## 6. Gameplay Flow
The player fights waves of enemies. The goal is the highest score, the progress is the meter, success is a full meter, failure is a broken chain and the next action is the next wave. What breaks the combo is not decided yet.

## 7. Variants
Single version

## 8. Interface and Feedback
One bar under the score.

## 9. Minimum Scope
### Must Have
- The bar; the filling and the emptying.
### Can Be Simplified
- The look of the bar.
### Out of Scope
- Rewards for a full meter.

## 10. How to Test
Six players, two rounds each, about ten minutes; notes are collected in a chat.

## 11. Success Criteria
Players chain more actions per wave (a preliminary acceptance target).

## 12. Risks
Without a clear rule for breaking the chain the result will be unclear.

## 13. Follow-up Decisions
Supported: keep the meter. Not Supported: drop it. Inconclusive: repeat after the rule is decided.
