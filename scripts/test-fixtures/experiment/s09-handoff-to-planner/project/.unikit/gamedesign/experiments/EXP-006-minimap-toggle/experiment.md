# EXP-006: Minimap toggle

## 1. Identification
- ID: EXP-006
- Title: Minimap toggle
- Summary: A switch that hides the minimap, to see whether players look at the world more.
- Category: interface
- Status: Ready for Planning
- Version: 1
- Created: 2026-10-01
- Updated: 2026-10-01
- Related: none
- Origin: none

## 2. Problem
Players stare at the minimap and miss the world around them.

## 3. Hypothesis
If players can hide the minimap, more of them look at the world, because the screen is less busy.

## 4. What We Test
Do players look at the world more when they can hide the minimap?

## 5. Design
A button in the corner hides or shows the minimap. In the other variant the minimap is always on.
The card describes a disposable prototype, not production-ready functionality.

## 6. Gameplay Flow
The player explores the level and may toggle the minimap at any time. The goal is the exit, the progress is the rooms found, success is the exit, failure is leaving the level and the next action is the next level.

## 7. Variants
A: minimap always on. B: a toggle

## 8. Interface and Feedback
One small button next to the minimap and the minimap itself.

## 9. Minimum Scope
### Must Have
- The toggle button; a switch between variant A and variant B.
### Can Be Simplified
- The icon of the button.
### Out of Scope
- Resizing the minimap; new map content.

## 10. How to Test
Six players, two rounds each, about ten minutes; notes are collected in a chat.

## 11. Success Criteria
Players in variant B can describe more of the level afterwards (a preliminary acceptance target).

## 12. Risks
Players may never press the button.

## 13. Follow-up Decisions
Supported: keep the toggle. Partially Supported: hide it by default. Not Supported: drop it. Inconclusive: repeat.
