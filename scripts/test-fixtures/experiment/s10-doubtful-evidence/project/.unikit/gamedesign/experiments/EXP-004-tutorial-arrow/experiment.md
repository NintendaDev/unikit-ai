# EXP-004: Tutorial arrow

## 1. Identification
- ID: EXP-004
- Title: Tutorial arrow
- Summary: An arrow that points at the next door in the first level, to see whether fewer players get lost.
- Category: onboarding
- Status: Testing
- Version: 1
- Created: 2026-10-01
- Updated: 2026-10-01
- Related: none
- Origin: none

## 2. Problem
Some players cannot find the next door in the first level and quit.

## 3. Hypothesis
If an arrow points at the next door, fewer players get lost in the first level, because the next step is visible.

## 4. What We Test
Does a pointing arrow reduce the time players spend lost in the first level?

## 5. Design
A bright arrow floats above the player and turns toward the next door until the player passes it.
The card describes a disposable prototype, not production-ready functionality.

## 6. Gameplay Flow
The player starts in the first room and follows the arrow. The goal is the exit, the progress is each door passed, success is the exit, failure is leaving the level and the next action is the second level.

## 7. Variants
Single version

## 8. Interface and Feedback
One arrow, bright against the floor, and nothing else added.

## 9. Minimum Scope
### Must Have
- The arrow; the turning toward the next door.
### Can Be Simplified
- The shape and colour of the arrow.
### Out of Scope
- A map; voice hints.

## 10. How to Test
Six players, one round each, about ten minutes; notes are collected in a chat.

## 11. Success Criteria
Fewer players report being lost than in the last build (a preliminary acceptance target).

## 12. Risks
Players who know the level will ignore the arrow.

## 13. Follow-up Decisions
Supported: keep the arrow. Partially Supported: test a path instead. Not Supported: look at the level layout. Inconclusive: repeat with new players.
