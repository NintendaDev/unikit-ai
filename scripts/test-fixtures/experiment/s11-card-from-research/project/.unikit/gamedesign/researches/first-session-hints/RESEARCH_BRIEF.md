# Research Brief: First session — a hint arrow toward the first locked door

- **Question**: how to make a new player understand, in the first two minutes, that a locked door opens by a nearby switch.
- **Key findings**:
  - The first room is closed and small; the switch is behind a low wall and is not in view from the door.
  - A hint arrow plus a glowing switch is the cheapest way to point at the switch.
  - Showing the action before the tool is a familiar way to teach without text.
- **Implications for our design**: the first goal of the session is clear at a glance; the pillar of calm exploration is tested by how long the player looks around before acting; the pillar of quick wins is tested by the door opening early.
- **Recommended follow-up**: build an experiment card from this research, then, after a first test with real players, decide whether the first session moves into the design documents.
