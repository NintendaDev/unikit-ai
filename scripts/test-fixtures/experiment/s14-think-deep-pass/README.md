# Scenario 14 — a deep pass for a new mechanic

## Scenario
The designer writes: "Think through an experiment for a new mechanic: the player pushes a heavy barrel along a track and the whole room reacts to where it rolls."

## Setup
No saved experiments. Web tools are available. The game has a name and numbers of its own that must not leave the project.

## Expected
- The depth question, with the deep pass recommended because the mechanic is new and adds several rules; the designer chooses deep.
- A frame as in the quick pass, then at most three precedents, each a row with the claim, its source, the date it was checked and a strength A, B or C; a claim without a source is marked as an assumption.
- Search queries that name the mechanic, the genre or the technique and never the name of the game, its numbers or its unreleased design; at most six searches or fetches in the pass; no page text pasted anywhere.
- Open questions closed one at a time, the recommended answer first; a deferred question goes to the notes as open and to the risks of the card.
- The notes are shown before the card draft and one approval covers both. After the answer the notes file is written first, then the card, the feedback file, the results file and the index last.
- The card carries a `Based on:` line of kind notes (title, path, date), and its Rationale holds each precedent in one line with its source.
- The planner, given the card, reads the notes in full and uses the frame, the risks of a misleading test and the questions.

## Must not
- Write the card before the notes file, or name a notes file that does not exist.
- Put the name of the game, its numbers or its unreleased design into a query.
- Present a claim without a source as a fact, or paste what a page says.
- Exceed three precedents or six searches.
- Write anything before approval.
